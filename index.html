About us

Barefoot Boxing is a not for profit boxing gym that offers training and support to all members of the Wagga community in a supportive environment. Every participant is given the same attention regardless of experience, fitness level or individual goals.

This gym is not just for competitive members. It is for anyone who wishes to gain fitness as well.

The gym is run on contribution fees from participants and sponsors. All contribution fees help cover the costs of the club and go straight back into club activities and equipment.

On Wiradjuri Country

Training

Who it is for

Everyone. All ages, all genders, all fitness levels and all experience, from first-timers to fighters. Every participant gets the same attention, and you don't have to compete to train here.

Sessions

Monday, 6:00pm to 8:00pm
Open to everyone. Fitness, or skills and partner work.

Tuesday, 6:00pm to 8:00pm
Open to everyone. Fitness, skills and partner work.

Wednesday, 6:00pm to 8:00pm: fighters night
Attendance requires weekly approval. Sparring, touch sparring, controlled or task sparring, padwork and tactical preparation.

Cost

$10 contribution, paid as you enter. The gym is run on contribution fees from participants and sponsors, all put back into the club.

What to bring

Anyone can come. Bring water and your boxing basics: your own gloves and hand wraps.

The rules

Be on time

Doors open 6:00pm. Start is 6:30pm. Doors shut at 6:25pm and there is no entry after that. No exceptions.

Not ready at 6:30pm? Sit and watch, or go home. Start means start: wraps on, toilet done, ready to go.

Turn up and put the work in

All three sessions matter. Skip them and you won't be put forward for sparring or fights.

Fights are also won outside the gym: running, sprints, shadow boxing, diet. It's you in the ring.
